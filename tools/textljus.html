Electrical: two ways to make it a second, controllable strip

A · Chain it after the band strip (recommended)

The band strip goes all the way round the core (488 mm) and its end lands back at the leaf tip, next to its start. Solder the text strip's DIN to the band strip's DOUT (plus 5 V and GND) and the firmware simply sees 30 + N LEDs: index 0–29 is the band, 30… is the text. No new wire down to the base, works for WS2812B, SK6812 and APA102 (CO/DO → CI/DI).

  band strip  DIN ◄── GPIO13 (as today)
       …30 LEDs around the core…
  band strip  DOUT ──► text strip DIN
              5V   ──► text strip 5V
              GND  ──► text strip GND

B · Its own data pin

Run one more data wire down the cable channel to a free pin, e.g. GPIO27 (WS2812B/SK6812), and register a second FastLED controller. Fully independent (own LED type, own refresh), but one more wire in the 7 mm channel and through the socket. Take 5 V/GND from the band strip's input pads.

  ESP32 GPIO13 ──[390Ω]──► band DIN   (unchanged)
  ESP32 GPIO27 ──[390Ω]──► text DIN   (new)
  band 5V/GND pads ──────► text 5V/GND

Power: 14–28 extra LEDs at full white is up to ~1.7 A more. FastLED's setMaxPowerInVoltsAndMilliamps() budget covers every controller, so LED_MAX_MILLIAMPS (3000 mA) still caps the whole lamp; the animations just get a bit dimmer at full white. In every variant the text strip sits inside the core, while the band strip is outside it, so the two light paths stay separate: the band and the text can do completely different things.
